## Long description (max 4000)

```
Lektra is a study planner built for the semester you actually have: lectures, sections, labs, quizzes, deadlines and exams in one calm place, stored on your device.

ADD YOUR TIMETABLE IN SECONDS
Photograph the sheet your department hands out and Lektra reads it into your week. Fix a row, save, and the week is there — no typing twenty entries by hand.

PLAN BY VOICE
On the way to class, say what you need to add. Lektra writes it down and puts it on the right day.

SEE THE WHOLE MONTH
A month grid with every class, quiz and deadline on its day. Colour-coded by subject, filtered in one tap.

FIND ANYTHING
One search field across every subject, class and deadline. Type a course code or "quiz" and it is there.

MAKE IT YOURS
Forty themes, light and dark, plus one you build. Change the month style, the event markers, the weekday labels and the text size until the page reads the way you do.

REMINDERS, YOUR WAY
Choose how far ahead Lektra nudges you for a class, a quiz or a deadline. Reminders are scheduled on your device — no account and no server in the middle.

MOVE IT WITH A FILE
Back up to one small file and restore it on another device. Your schedule stays yours to carry.

BUILT TO STAY PRIVATE
- Your subjects, lectures, files and settings are stored on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no ads.
- No ad networks, marketing pixels or session recording.
- Scanning a timetable photo and the in-app chat are optional. When you use them, that photo or message is sent to a third-party AI service to produce the result. Everything else works without them, fully offline.

FREE
Lektra is free to use: no subscription, no locked features, no upsell.

A NOTE ON INSTALLING
Lektra installs from Google Play and runs full screen like any other app.

Questions, bugs or feedback: ahmed.kamalrezkk@gmail.com
Privacy policy: https://ahmedkamalrezk.github.io/Lektra-web/privacy/
```

## Still needed in Play Console

- **Feature graphic** — 1024 × 500 PNG or JPEG, no transparency. Not included
  here yet.
- **App icon** — 512 × 512 PNG, 32-bit. `store/play/icon-512.png` is the site
  icon at the right size, but its rounded corners are baked in with
  transparency; Play applies its own mask, so a full-bleed square export looks
  better. Worth re-exporting from the source artwork.
- **Phone screenshots** — the eight above. Play accepts 2–8, so you can drop
  the last one if you prefer seven.
- **Tablet screenshots** — optional but recommended; Play shows the listing on
  tablets and a 7-inch and 10-inch set is worth adding later.
